Diagnosis

Most businesses run search as two silos. Google runs it as one page.

The expensive problems are rarely inside either channel. They sit in the seam between them — which is exactly where nobody is looking.

Paying for clicks you already earn.

Ad budget quietly bids on terms the site already ranks for, because nobody checks the overlap.

Tracking is lying.

Duplicated or missing conversions poison both channels — Google optimizes ads and content toward bad data.

Rankings that don't pay rent.

Organic traffic lands on pages that can't convert; content chases volume instead of buyers.

Two vendors, no strategy.

The ads person and the SEO person have never spoken. The results page doesn't care about your org chart.

FAQ

Straight answers before you send access.

Is the free leak check actually free?

Yes. No card, no call, no trial that becomes a bill. One channel, five a week, and the scorecard is yours either way. It tells you whether you're leaking and roughly how much — the audit tells you where it's coming from and what to fix first.

Do I have to do both channels?

No — plenty of accounts start with one. The bundle exists because paid queries reveal what content should target, and rankings tell ads what not to buy. Each stands alone.

Do I need to commit to monthly management?

No. Audits and cleanups are one-time projects. Management is month-to-month, cancel anytime.

What access do you need?

Read-only Google Ads, GA4 and Search Console. Admin only if you want changes implemented. You stay the owner and can revoke anytime.

How fast does organic actually move?

Slower than ads. Technical fixes can show in weeks; content compounds over months. The roadmap separates quick wins from long bets so you know which is which.

What about AI Overviews, ChatGPT & Perplexity?

Built into organic and combined engagements, never billed separately. Models cite sources they can parse and trust, which is the same structured data and authority your organic work already builds.

How is this different from an agency?

Agencies bundle search into broad retainers with multiple handoffs. Clarify is narrower, and you work with the person doing the work.

Do you guarantee results or rankings?

No — nobody controls Google, and anyone who guarantees otherwise is selling something. The goal is better account quality and honest measurement.

Hire Clarify or use the software?

Software keeps you informed. Management gets it handled. The tool flags what's wrong; hiring Clarify means someone decides what to do about it and does it.
